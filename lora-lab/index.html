B starts at exactly zero, so ΔW starts at zero and the fine-tuned model begins life behaving identically to the pretrained one. That is the whole trick: LoRA is a no-op at initialisation, so you can never make the model worse by attaching an untrained adapter.

The perfect update ΔW* breaks into 8 independent directions of decreasing importance. A rank-2 adapter can represent the first 2 of them and no more. The squared lengths of the greyed-out bars are exactly the floor drawn on the loss chart — that is not an empirical observation, it's the Eckart–Young theorem, and the training run above lands on it to five decimal places.
