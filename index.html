Web Projects

A collection of small web experiments

  • 📄 Substack Reader Extract clean article text from any Substack URL
  • 🗺️ Let's Go Walk-based board games with GPS movement, map overlay, and AI
  • 🪄 YAML Slides Write a tiny slideshow in YAML and share the full deck in the URL
  • 🧩 Puzzlegame 3D first-person puzzles — every level has its own visual world and its own mechanic
  • 🎧 Audio Transcribe PWA — share an audio recording from Android and get a Groq Whisper transcript
  • 🔗 URL Opener PWA — open a clipboard or shared URL with a custom prefix, installable on Android
  • 📋 Text Share PWA — receive shared URLs and text from Android, collect them in a clipboard
