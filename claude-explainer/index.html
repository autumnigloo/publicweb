How Claudes Are Made

A visual walkthrough of the research, data, and training that turns raw compute into a helpful AI assistant.

Step 1 — Data

Start with the world's text

Trillions of tokens from the open web, digitized books, scientific papers, and code repositories are carefully filtered and deduplicated.

The model is a Transformer — stacks of attention layers trained on thousands of GPUs for weeks. It learns grammar, facts, reasoning, and code all from the same signal: predict the next token.

Step 2 — Tokens

What is a token?

Text is split into subword tokens (roughly ¾ of a word each). The model sees a context window of thousands of tokens and learns to predict the next one — repeated hundreds of billions of times.

Step 3 — Alignment

Teaching Claude to be helpful

1
Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) — Human trainers write ideal responses; the model learns to imitate them.
2
Reward Model — Trainers rank pairs of responses; a separate model learns what "better" looks like.
3
RL Fine-Tuning (PPO) — The language model is optimised to score highly on the reward model.

This process is called RLHF — Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback.

Step 4 — Constitutional AI

A set of principles, not just examples

Claude's Constitution includes principles like …

  • Be helpful, harmless, and honest
  • Respect human autonomy and dignity
  • Avoid deception and manipulation
  • Support human oversight of AI

Anthropic's Constitutional AI method uses the written principles to generate AI feedback — the model critiques and revises its own outputs against the constitution, reducing reliance on human labelling for safety.
