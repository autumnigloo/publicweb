How to play

🎯 Aim: drag across the board to swing Soko's cannon, or tap where you want it pointed. ◀ ▶ nudge a tenth of a degree.

👀 The dotted guide follows the marble past walls, chutes and bumpers up to the first thing it changes. A green arrow and dashed box show where a crate will go; ✕ means it's blocked.

📦 A marble that hits a crate's side shoves it one tile away. Corner hits and soft hits don't move it. Each marble moves each crate at most once.

◆ Crates lock onto the glowing pads. Fill every pad to win.

🔵 Pegs light up and vanish after the shot. Crates flatten pegs they slide onto. Gold pegs pay bolts, green pegs charge a power marble.

↶ Undo is free and unlimited: experiment!
